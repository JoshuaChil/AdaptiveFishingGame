Controls

Place a part
Click a palette card, then click on the board, or drag the card onto the board. Green ghost = valid, red = invalid.
Move a part
Drag it with the Select tool. Through-hole parts snap to holes; the XIAO straddles the channel.
Wire
Press W (or the Wire tool), click the first hole or terminal, then click the second. Esc cancels. Drag a wire's middle to bend it.
Bridge
Press B, then click two adjacent holes to solder-bridge them.
Nets
Hover any hole, pin or terminal to highlight everything connected to it; the status bar shows the net name.
Rotate
R rotates the selected part 90° around its anchor pin.
Delete
Delete or Backspace removes the selection (a part's wires go with it).
Undo / Redo
Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z.
Pan
Hold Space and drag, or use the Pan tool.
Zoom
Mouse wheel / trackpad scroll over the canvas.
Tools
V Select, W Wire, B Bridge, Esc back to Select.
Run mode
Press Run, then toggle GPIO pins or start a script in the Run tab. Blue = GND, red = VCC, orange = HIGH.
